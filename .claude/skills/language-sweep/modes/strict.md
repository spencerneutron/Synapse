# Strict mode: ASD-STE100

Rewrite the supplied text to follow these rules.

1. One instruction per sentence. Maximum 20 words in procedural text,
   25 in descriptive text.
2. Maximum 6 sentences per paragraph. Maximum 3 in a warning or caution.
3. Active voice. Name the actor.
4. Simple present tense or imperative. No perfect or progressive tenses.
5. Keep articles. Do not drop "the" or "a".
6. No -ing forms as nouns or modifiers. Rewrite as a clause with a
   finite verb.
7. One word for one meaning. Do not use a noun as a verb.
8. Maximum 3 words in a noun cluster.
9. Put a warning or caution before the step it applies to. Write it as
   a command.
10. Reuse the same term for the same thing. Do not vary it for style.

## Register

Replace formal or Latinate verbs and connectives with their short
Germanic equivalents. Illustrative, not exhaustive: utilize to use,
ensure to make sure, perform to do, commence to start, terminate to
stop, prior to to before, in the event that to if, obtain to get,
approximately to about, provide to give.

When unsure whether a word has a simpler equivalent, keep the original.

## Order

Split sentences, then convert voice and tense, then adjust register.
Register changes made before splitting produce worse splits.

## Terms kept

If you keep a formal word because no simpler equivalent preserves the
meaning, list it under "Terms kept" after the rewritten text. Omit the
section if the list is empty.
