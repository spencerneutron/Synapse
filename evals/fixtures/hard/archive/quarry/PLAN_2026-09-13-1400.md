# PLAN — warehouse sync hardening

## Purpose
Make the nightly orders export to the warehouse trustworthy after the
2026-09-12 incident, when finance found a day of orders missing from the
warehouse reports.

## Constraints
- No schema changes on the orders database this quarter.
- The export must finish inside the 02:00-04:00 window.
- Keep `export.py` dependency-free (stdlib plus the in-repo `warehouse` client).

## Approach
1. Add logging around every warehouse call so the next incident leaves a trail.
2. Move the warehouse URL out of the code into `config.toml`.
3. Replay the 2026-09-12 export against the staging warehouse and compare
   row counts table by table.
4. Fix whatever the replay shows.

## Decision Log
- 2026-09-13: logging goes to the existing job log, not a new file; ops
  already ships that log to the dashboard.
- 2026-09-13: we replay against staging, never production, because the
  warehouse has no delete endpoint we are allowed to use.
