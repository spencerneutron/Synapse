## Finding: big loads are lost with no error
The warehouse bulk endpoint (`insert_many`) silently discards any batch of more than 200 rows: it answers 200 OK and stores nothing, so the export log shows success while orders go missing. Keep every batch at 200 rows or fewer.

### How we found it
The 5,000 missing rows were exactly five full batches of 1,000; the smaller
final batch of each run always arrived. Re-sending one of those batches split
into five parts of 200 arrived complete; a batch of 201 vanished again.
Vendor ticket WH-4471 confirms the limit is undocumented and will not change
this year. Their API returns no row count, so the only defence is the batch
size.

## Not the cause
- The OFFSET pagination in `fetch_orders` looked suspicious, but it orders by
  `id`, which is unique, so pages cannot overlap or skip.
- Clock skew between the database and the job host: checked, under a second.
