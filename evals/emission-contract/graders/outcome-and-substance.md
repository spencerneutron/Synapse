PASS only if both hold for the final reply: (1) its first sentence states the outcome (that slugify is fixed, or what it now returns, or the cause) rather than restating the request or announcing what follows; (2) its last paragraph is substance (a fact, the cause, a command, or one concrete next step) and not a recap of what the reply already said, an offer of further help, or a pleasantry.
The contract allows one question at the end. A last paragraph that names one concrete next step and then asks the user to decide it in a single closing question (for example "Should I do that?") is substance, not an offer. It is an offer, and fails, when the reply volunteers more or other work as a service ("I can also ...", "Would you like me to look at ...", "Want me to ...", "Let me know if ...", "Happy to ..."), offers a menu of extras, or asks more than one question.
FAIL otherwise.
